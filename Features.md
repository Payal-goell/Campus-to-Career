# Campus to Career — Features

This document lists the planned features of Campus to Career and
separates the first working version (MVP) from enhancements that can be
added later.

## 1. Public Website

- Home page introducing Campus to Career, its purpose, and benefits.
- Public overview of platform features and how it works.
- Explore public information about careers, branches, colleges, and
  learning resources.
- Login and account registration entry points.
- Forgot-password and account-recovery flow.

## 2. Student Account and Profile

- Student registration and login.
- Email verification where supported by the selected authentication
  setup.
- Profile creation and editing.
- Academic details: college, branch, course, year of study, and
  graduation year.
- Interests, current skills, desired skills, career goals, projects,
  certifications, and achievements.
- Profile-completion indicator.
- Secure access so students can view and update their own private
  information.

## 3. Student Dashboard

- Personalized overview after login.
- Current career goal and academic-year summary.
- Profile completion and development progress.
- Roadmap milestones and their status.
- Saved learning resources.
- Upcoming college events and opportunities.
- Recent activities, notifications, and reminders.
- Quick links to the main platform modules.

## 4. Course and Career Exploration

- Browse courses, branches, career paths, and job roles.
- View descriptions, typical responsibilities, required skills, and
  suggested learning paths.
- Search and filter options by interest, skill, field, or career.
- Save courses and career options for later.
- Compare selected options to support informed decisions.
- Show suggestions based on information the student has provided, where
  supported.

## 5. College Events, Clubs, and Committees

- List college events, workshops, hackathons, competitions, clubs, and
  committees.
- Display event recaps, photos or media when available, and key
  takeaways.
- Explain club and committee purposes, roles, responsibilities, and
  participation requirements.
- Show upcoming dates, eligibility, and registration details where
  available.
- Connect activities to skills students may develop, such as
  communication, teamwork, leadership, and technical skills.
- Display the relevant student coordinator’s name, role, and approved
  contact method.

## 6. Resource Manager

- Centralized catalogue of learning resources.
- Include free and paid courses, videos, books, documentation,
  certifications, and practice platforms.
- Categorize resources by skill, topic, career path, difficulty, format,
  and academic year.
- Display resource name, provider, description, level, cost label, and
  original provider link.
- Clearly distinguish free resources from paid resources.
- Allow students to bookmark resources.
- Allow students to mark resources as not started, in progress, or
  completed.
- Provide search and filters to reduce confusion and make resources
  easier to find.

## 7. Personalized Year-Wise Roadmap

- Create a roadmap based on the student’s year, interests, skills, and
  career goal.
- Allow students to update their goals as their interests change.
- Display milestones, recommended activities, learning resources, and
  target outcomes.
- Let students mark milestones as not started, in progress, or
  completed.
- Show progress toward roadmap goals.

### Suggested year-wise guidance

- **First Year — Explore and Build Foundations:** explore careers,
  identify interests, learn fundamentals, and discover college
  activities.
- **Second Year — Build and Experiment:** develop core skills, complete
  small projects, participate in hackathons, and begin certifications.
- **Third Year — Specialize and Prepare:** work on stronger projects,
  pursue internships, improve aptitude, and build industry-relevant
  skills.
- **Fourth Year — Placement Readiness:** refine the resume, practice
  interviews, apply for jobs, and track applications.

The roadmap should be adaptable to each student’s goals rather than
identical for everyone.

## 8. Extracurricular Development

- Discover clubs, committees, hackathons, competitions, sports, cultural
  activities, and leadership opportunities.
- Filter opportunities by category and skills of interest.
- Explain the skills and experience each activity may help develop.
- Save activities and record participation.
- Record achievements, responsibilities, and outcomes.
- Include extracurricular progress in the student’s overall development
  summary.

## 9. Placement Preparation

- Placement preparation checklist and learning plan.
- Resume-building guidance and templates.
- Aptitude and technical preparation resources.
- Interview preparation, including technical, HR, and behavioral topics.
- Project and certification tracking.
- Internship and job opportunity listings where available.
- Student-managed application tracker with stages such as saved,
  applied, interview, offer, and closed.
- Record placement outcomes and skills used.
- Clearly identify student-entered information versus college-verified
  information.

## 10. Student Coordinator Directory

- Search or browse coordinators by activity, club, committee, or
  responsibility.
- Display name, role, responsibility area, and approved contact method.
- Provide clear guidance about which coordinator to contact for a
  particular question.
- Publish only contact details approved for sharing.

## 11. Development Score and Analytics

- Summarize completed roadmap milestones, skills, projects,
  certifications, extracurricular participation, and placement
  preparation.
- Display progress over time using understandable indicators.
- Explain the inputs used to calculate any overall development score.
- Allow students to review and correct their records.
- Treat the score as a progress indicator, not a definitive judgment of
  student ability.

## 12. Notifications

- Notify students about relevant upcoming events and opportunities.
- Remind students about roadmap milestones they have chosen.
- Show profile or application updates where applicable.
- Let students view recent notifications in the dashboard.
- Avoid sending unnecessary or excessive notifications.

## 13. College Administrator Features

- Secure administrator login and role-based access.
- Add, edit, publish, and archive events and recaps.
- Manage branch, course, club, committee, and opportunity information.
- Add and update resources and their free/paid labels.
- Manage coordinator directory entries.
- View appropriate platform usage and progress reports.
- Prevent unauthorized users from accessing administration tools.

## 14. Future Enhancements

These features should be considered only after the core platform is
working reliably: - AI-assisted career and resource recommendations. -
AI-guided roadmap adjustments based on student progress. - Resume
feedback and interview-practice assistance. - Recruiter accounts and
verified job postings. - Automated skill-gap analysis. - College ERP or
placement-system integration. - Advanced analytics and aggregated
reports. - Additional accessibility and language options.

## 15. MVP Priority

### Must Have — First Working Version

1.  Student registration, login, and profile.
2.  Student dashboard.
3.  Course and career exploration.
4.  Events, clubs, committees, and recaps.
5.  Resource manager with free/paid labels and external links.
6.  Personalized year-wise roadmap with milestone tracking.
7.  Extracurricular opportunity listings.
8.  Student coordinator directory.
9.  Basic placement preparation and application tracking.
10. Basic development summary.
11. Administrator tools to manage platform content.

### Should Have — After the Core MVP

- Notifications and reminders.
- Advanced search and filtering.
- Saved comparisons for careers and courses.
- More detailed analytics and reports.

### Could Have — Later Versions

- AI-powered recommendations and resume feedback.
- Recruiter portal.
- Automated integrations with college systems.

## 16. General Feature Rules

- Features should work on desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- Forms should validate input and explain errors clearly.
- Personal student information must be protected.
- Administrative actions must require appropriate permissions.
- External resources should link to their original providers.
- Paid resources must be labelled clearly.
- Recommendations and development scores should be transparent and
  should not promise placements or guaranteed career outcomes.
